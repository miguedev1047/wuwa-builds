Pros & Cons
Pros

  • Self-sufficient: heals herself, applies and amplifies Aero Erosion, making her able to exploit her kit by herself (albeit not to its fullest potential);

  • Massive room for optimization through quickswap - none of her teammates have any issues when it comes to utilizing the mechanic to its fullest potential, meaning her ceiling is much higher than her baseline performance;

  • High sequence scaling, particularly her S2 and S3 offer great benefits that alleviate her otherwise somewhat strict teambuilding conditions;

  • Possesses interruption resistance, grouping and stagnating, Increases teammate interruption resistance & healing, funnels Forte points to Aero Rover if present — makes team rotations much smoother;

  • INSANE second Ultimate animation.

Cons

  • HP scaling means alternative weapon options to her Signature really fall behind. Overall one of the most Signature weapon-reliant characters in the game;

  • In multi-wave content, non-negotiably requires her ideal teammate Ciaccona in order to function correctly - not even ideal in gamemodes like Whimpering Wastes, but to use her there Ciaccona is not skippable;

  • Requires a lot of optimization in her rotations to function without Ciaccona in any gamemode in general, Ciaccona being a character who is very difficult to acquire nowadays due to a lack of reruns.

Review

Cartethyia is a 5-star Aero Sword DPS character who relies on the Aero Erosion mechanic to take her damage to the next level. Defined by a very Quickswap-friendly kit and a very flexible rotation with many tools at her disposal, breaking her kit down requires some much needed simplification. We’ll try to provide as concise and clear of an explanation as we can throughout this review.Key Mechanics

Something to note to start with, is that the entirety of Cartethyia’s damage is based on her HP. This makes building her certainly original, but also way more restrictive, as most Swords and Echo sets revolve around buffing ATK. This makes her a very selective DPS in general and is one of her main flaws - but we’re getting ahead of ourselves, so let’s start with her kit in the order of her rotation:

First of all, Cartethyia has two forms. Her Ultimate allows her to transform into Fleurdelys to gain a new kit, but before that, she needs to accumulate what’s known as “Sword Shadows”. There are 3 types of Sword Shadows, all of which enhance her kit in her Fleurdelys form where she will deal the overwhelming majority of her damage. All Sword Shadows acquired are visible on her Forte bar in her Cartethyia form. Their effects to Fleurdelys are as follows:

  • Sword of Discord: Gained after using Cartethyia’s Intro Skill or Heavy Attack, it raises the Aero Erosion stacks on all nearby enemies to whatever count is highest among the currently remaining enemies after using most of Fleurdelys’s important abilities. This has two effects: the first is keeping all enemies in range at the same amount of Aero Erosion, the second is refreshing the duration of Aero Erosion (so the stacks will never expire mid-rotation). However, it’s worth noting this does not help her in multi-wave content;

  • Sword of Divinity: Gained after using Cartethyia’s Basic Attack 4, it amplifies Aero Erosion DMG by 50% and reduces the damage interval by 50% for enemies near Fleurdelys (meaning the damage is applied twice as fast effectively doubling the damage on top of 50% Amplification);

  • Sword of Virtue: Gained after using Cartethyia’s Skill, it allows her to gain increased Interruption Resistance and generate a stagnation field after using her Basic Attack 4 in Fleurdelys form.

Another effect stacking up each Sword will have is enhancing Cartethyia’s Mid-Air Attack. Using this Mid-Air Attack summons all Sword Shadows to attack the enemy, inflicting high damage and granting their effects to Fleurdelys. Hence, it is mandatory to cast this Mid-Air Attack every rotation to ensure Fleurdelys is as buffed as can be to deal the highest amount of damage possible. Besides, the damage on this ability is high, so increasing it further isn’t a bad idea in any context.

Aero Erosion and its effects

Cartethyia’s Intro Skill and Resonance Skill each apply 2 stacks of Aero Erosion on the targets hit, while Basic Attack 4 applies 1 stack, for a total of 5 Aero Erosion stacks per rotation. The limit is naturally 3 stacks, however this can be increased via Aero Rover or Chisa’s Outro skills and via Cartethyia’s Sequence 2 (each increasing the limit by 3). At most, Fleurdelys benefits from 6 Aero Erosion stacks being present on her targets. Via Cartethyia’s Inherent Skill, all damage dealt by Cartethyia and Fleurdelys on targets affected by Aero Erosion increases by 30% regardless of their stack count. For every stack above 3, she also gains an extra 10% Aero Erosion up to 30% at 6 stacks (for a total of 60% DMG Bonus). This gives her a tremendous amount of DMG Bonus, further adding on to the necessity to apply Aero Erosion on her targets.

Aero Erosion will periodically deal some decent damage to your enemies throughout your team’s rotation, and will also be directly consumed by Cartethyia once she transforms into Fleurdelys to enhance her attacks.

Cartethyia’s Ultimate: Fleurdelys form

Upon using her Ultimate, Cartethyia transforms into Fleurdelys. This makes her gain the “Manifest” state which grants the following effects:

  • When transforming into Fleurdelys, Cartethyia immediately reduces her Max HP to 50% of its original value (unless her current HP is already equal to or below 50%). The Manifest state lasts for 12s.

  • Fleurdelys gains a brand new kit with new Basic Attacks, 2 new Heavy Attacks, 2 new Resonance Skills, a new Forte bar called “Conviction” which allows her to cast her second Ultimate (the hardest-hitting ability she has), a brand new Intro Skill and certain attacks make her consume Aero Erosion instead of applying it.

  • When using several key abilities, Fleurdelys will consume 1 stack of Aero Erosion to instantly deal the instance of damage it normally would’ve done.

  • When using any ability, Fleurdelys restores a portion of Conviction, stackable up to 120. At 120 points, she is able to cast her second Ultimate, deal massive damage in this process, and end her rotation.

  • The damage of this second Ultimate is influenced by how much Aero Erosion is present on the targets, up to 5 stacks. Each of these 5 potential stacks get removed on cast and amplify the damage of the attack by 20% each (up to 100%).

  • The second Ultimate restores 50% of Cartethyia’s (not Fleurdelys’s) max HP.

Lastly, her Outro amplifies Aero DMG dealt to targets with Negative Status applied by 17.5% for 20s by allies other than herself, which is an acceptable boost but nothing impressive.

Meta Position & Conclusion

All of Cartethyia’s kit mechanics tied to Aero Erosion consumption aren’t utilizable to their fullest on her own. She lacks enough Aero Erosion application to do so before S3, and before S2 she cannot naturally increase the stack cap of Aero Erosion on the targets. This makes someone who has that ability, either Chisa or Aero Rover, ideal. She will need to slow her rotation down, or be paired with extra sources of Aero Erosion application (ideally Ciaccona) to take full advantage of her kit. 

At S0, Cartethyia’s teams do get restricted by the nature of her kit’s mechanics, as they tie her down to specific Aero Erosion teammates. However, even without Ciaccona available, considering Aero Rover is a free character who can increase the stack cap of Aero Erosion on targets, Cartethyia does get fantastic damage on her own, as her kit is mostly self-sufficient. Add that to an insane affinity for Quickswap (more than basically any other character in the game), and overall you are left with a pretty complete character, although her damage ceiling is expectedly not up to the standard of her most modern DPS peers. However, using her fullest quickswap potential, she can get very close, and that’s a testament to her affinity for the mechanic.

In conclusion, Cartethyia is a good all-around damage dealer to this day. Her affinity for quickswap provides her a long standing in the upcoming meta for people who wish to optimize her to the fullest. In the hands of the average person, she remains a decent choice for a DPS, particularly if her Sequence nodes are unlocked, although she wouldn’t be a recommended pull for new players today. Having inevitably fallen behind the most modern DPS characters of Wuthering Waves, Cartethyia nonetheless remains a capable damage dealer who can provide some good results under the right conditions, and some amazing results in the right quickswappy hands.

Additional Tips & Tricks

The listed animation cancels used in the rotations above occur as follows

Cartethyia Basic 4: As soon as it is cast

Fleurdelys  Basic 5: When she drops her sword on enemies (sword starts falling from the sky)

Cartethyia Mid-air Attack: When Cartethyia lands

Cartethyia Basic 4 —> Heavy: It’s possible to hold Heavy on the same input as Basic 4.

In Quickswap, Cartethyia has many good swap cancel windows. In Cartethyia form, her Basic 3, 4, Skill, and Mid-air Attack all have their niche use cases. In Fleurdelys form, her Skill 2 and Basic 4/5 are the main ones used. Her best team is fully quickswap-friendly, so many team-specific quickswap combos are possible.

Synergies
  • Ciaccona - The best Sub DPS option for Cartethyia, who has a short rotation whilst doing great damage, provides good buffs via her Solo Concert, Echo set and potential weapon, and amplifies Aero Erosion damage by 100% on Outro. But most importantly, Ciaccona applies a ton of Aero Erosion off-field over time, which allows Cartethyia to make full leverage of her kit buffs. For these reasons, they are best when paired together.
  • Rover (Aero) Chisa The Shorekeeper - All of the three best third slot Support choices Cartethyia has. While Ciaccona is consistently on all of her best teams, each of these characters have their use cases. Aero Rover and Chisa are built to make full leverage of Cartethyia's kit since they can each increase the Aero Erosion stack count on enemies by 3; Shorekeeper is mostly used in Whimpering Wastes since ramping Aero Erosion up to 6 is difficult in multi-wave content so her buffs and rotation speed can see use. While Chisa has better buffs than Aero Rover, that's only with her Signature weapon equipped. Pick and choose your support based on your situation, and you can always play 2 of them as an alternative if Ciaccona isn't available!
  • Sanhua - Sanhua works with Cartethyia as her best F2P pair, because buffing Basic Attack DMG for her isn't too bad as it buffs a number of her abilities, but most importantly Sanhua rotates in and out at lightspeed, while still being able to provide some buffing via her Echo set. As she doesn't apply any Aero Erosion, Sanhua's main purpose is mostly to get in and get out for Cartethyia to take all available field time. For these reasons, it's an acceptable duo, still stronger than alternatives like Aalto as he doesn't back up his field time with enough damage and barely gives any higher buffing potential.
